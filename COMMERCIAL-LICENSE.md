# Architectonic Commercial Licensing Framework

This document describes available commercial licensing pathways for Architectonic. It is not itself a binding license, quote, offer, or contract.

All commercial rights require a separate written agreement with Apex Governance Group.

## Licensing pathways

### 1. Professional / Internal Commercial License
For one organization using Architectonic internally for business operations.

Typical scope may include:
- internal production use;
- named users or approved business units;
- approved deployment environment;
- defined support and update rights;
- restrictions on redistribution and resale.

### 2. Enterprise License
For broader multi-team, multi-business-unit, or enterprise deployment.

Typical scope may include:
- enterprise-wide internal use;
- governed template libraries;
- advanced decision-intelligence capabilities;
- human-system and negotiation modules;
- analytics and calibration components;
- enterprise governance and assurance tooling;
- approved integrations and support.

### 3. Managed Service / Consulting License
For service providers or integrators using Architectonic to deliver services to clients.

This requires express permission because client-facing use is prohibited under the public evaluation license.

Terms may address:
- client count;
- project count;
- territory;
- revenue share or annual fee;
- data boundaries;
- derivative deliverables;
- support and assurance responsibilities.

### 4. OEM / Embedded License
For embedding Architectonic capability into another commercial product, platform, appliance, or service.

Terms may address:
- embedding rights;
- API/runtime rights;
- branding;
- attribution;
- redistribution;
- minimum commitments;
- support;
- update and security obligations.

### 5. Government / Regulated Environment License
For public-sector, defense, regulated, or controlled environments.

This pathway requires environment-specific review and does not imply certification, authorization to operate, compliance, or security accreditation.

Terms may address:
- deployment boundary;
- data handling;
- records and retention;
- approved integrations;
- support;
- independent assurance;
- customer-specific authorization requirements.

### 6. Research / Academic Commercial Exception
For universities, labs, or funded research programs whose use exceeds the public noncommercial evaluation grant.

Terms may address:
- publication;
- derivative research artifacts;
- attribution;
- redistribution;
- model-training restrictions;
- sponsor rights.

## Rights that always require written authorization

The following are never granted merely by public access, donation, sponsorship, fork, or contribution:

- production commercial use;
- resale or sublicensing;
- managed services;
- white-label deployment;
- OEM embedding;
- commercial redistribution;
- commercial model training;
- customer deployment;
- government/regulated production use;
- use of private Architectonic Enterprise assets.

## Commercial engagement structure

A commercial agreement may include:
- scope and authorized users;
- deployment environment;
- support level;
- maintenance and update rights;
- data rights;
- confidentiality;
- audit rights;
- security obligations;
- acceptance criteria;
- liability allocation;
- termination and transition;
- pricing and payment terms;
- renewal;
- professional services.

## Design-partner pilots

A paid design-partner pilot may be used to evaluate fit before broader licensing.

A pilot may include:
- discovery;
- baseline assessment;
- architecture mapping;
- human-system analysis;
- COA analysis;
- implementation roadmap;
- measurement plan;
- bounded intervention;
- outcome review.

Pilot participation does not automatically create enterprise, redistribution, or perpetual rights.
