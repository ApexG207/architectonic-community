# Architectonic Design-Partner Intake

Use this worksheet to determine whether a bounded Architectonic pilot is appropriate. Keep responses public and non-sensitive.

## Organization
Name, sector, jurisdiction, and operating context.

## Decision / Problem
What decision, organizational problem, or transformation challenge needs improvement?

## Beneficiary
Who benefits if the decision or process improves?

## Decision owner / authority
Who has actual authority to approve the pilot, intervention, and resulting decision?

## Current baseline
Describe current performance, decision latency, rework, fragmentation, risk, burden, or other observable conditions.

## Functions affected
Business units, programs, teams, partners, systems, or stakeholders involved.

## Evidence available
Authoritative records, process data, interviews, surveys, measures, logs, or other evidence sources.

## Desired effect

What observable change would make the pilot worthwhile?

## Success thresholds

Quality, time, cost, risk, adoption, decision-usefulness, or other explicit thresholds.

## Proposed pilot / intervention

What bounded pilot or intervention would be used to test the desired effect, and what is explicitly out of scope?

## Pilot and data-use authority

Who has authority to approve the proposed pilot, intervention, and use of the relevant data? Identify any required organizational, contractual, ethics, security, records, or regulatory approvals.

## Time horizon

Desired pilot start, duration, and observation period, including when outcomes will be measured.

## Deployment environment
Public cloud, commercial enterprise, regulated environment, government environment, isolated network, or other.

## Data boundary
Identify privacy, security, contractual, export, records, client, government, or controlled-data restrictions.

## Reversibility
Can the proposed intervention be safely reversed if results are poor?

## Independent observation

Who can observe and adjudicate outcomes independently of the builder, and what outcome-review process will be used?

## Budget / procurement
Known budget range, procurement path, or constraints.

## Contact and next step

An inquiry does not authorize deployment, field intervention, certification, commercial use, or entry into calibration. Use official Apex Governance Group channels for protected or confidential details; do not post confidential, controlled, client, personal, or nonpublic government information in a public issue.
