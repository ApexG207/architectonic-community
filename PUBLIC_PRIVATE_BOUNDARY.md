# Architectonic Public / Proprietary Boundary

Architectonic operates as two deliberately separated surfaces.

## Public surface

Purpose:
- explain the product;
- demonstrate concepts;
- provide synthetic examples;
- support evaluation and design-partner discovery.

Public assets may include:
- Community Preview repository;
- public SharePoint demo;
- synthetic Northstar scenario;
- public schemas;
- public documentation;
- demo decision packages;
- simplified measures and QAQC examples.

## Proprietary surface

Purpose:
- run actual client/internal pilots;
- preserve proprietary methods and implementation;
- support governed deployment, measurement, calibration, assurance, and release.

Proprietary assets include:
- full template catalog;
- private Builder and meta-architecture;
- advanced orchestration;
- calibration and benchmark assets;
- premium human-system / negotiation / advantage models;
- production QAQC and release controls;
- customer-specific connectors;
- regulated-environment profiles;
- client graphs and restricted reference libraries.

## Transfer control

Enterprise -> Public:
Only explicit, sanitized, releasable, synthetic or approved content may move outward.

Public -> Enterprise:
Public feedback or demo artifacts are non-authoritative until provenance is checked, requirements are validated, and an approved lifecycle transition occurs.

## Hard rule

The public demo must be useful enough to understand Architectonic while remaining intentionally incapable of reproducing the complete Enterprise implementation.
