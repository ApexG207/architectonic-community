# Contributing

Architectonic Community Preview is source-available, not open source.

Contributions may include:
- documentation corrections;
- schema improvements;
- synthetic examples;
- reproducible bug reports;
- interoperability suggestions;
- public evaluation feedback.

Do not submit:
- customer data;
- nonpublic government information;
- credentials or secrets;
- controlled or regulated data;
- third-party confidential material;
- material you do not have authority to contribute.

Submission does not automatically grant commercial rights to Architectonic or obligate Apex Governance Group to accept, use, compensate, or publish a contribution. Material contributions may require a separate Contributor License Agreement.

Before contributing, review LICENSE.md, TRADEMARKS.md, SECURITY.md, and NOTICE.md.
