# Public Pilot Measurement Framework

## Measures of Performance (MOP)
- authority verified;
- decision contract completed;
- baseline frozen;
- forecast issued prospectively;
- intervention recorded;
- observation window completed;
- outcome adjudicated;
- calibration record created.

## Measures of Effectiveness (MOE)
- decision latency change;
- rework reduction;
- evidence traceability;
- decision usefulness;
- adoption;
- burden reduction;
- stakeholder alignment;
- observed effect against baseline.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPI)
- time-to-architecture;
- time-to-decision;
- recommendation / abstention rate;
- forecast calibration;
- pilot completion rate;
- implementation hours;
- cost-to-pilot;
- time-to-value;
- design-partner-to-commercial conversion.

## Guardrails
- no synthetic outcome enters field calibration;
- pending or unobservable outcomes are not failures;
- no unsupported causal claim;
- no authority inference from a completed form;
- no demographic or cultural variable used as a deterministic proxy for individual intent, competence, trust, or risk.
