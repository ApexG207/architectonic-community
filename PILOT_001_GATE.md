# Empirical Pilot 001 — Entry Gate

**Status:** HOLD — QUALIFIED DESIGN PARTNER NOT YET IDENTIFIED

A real field pilot must not begin from a synthetic or assumed customer.

## Mandatory entry evidence
- named participating organization;
- actual decision/problem;
- beneficiary;
- accountable decision owner;
- valid pilot/intervention authority;
- data-use authority and boundary;
- current-state baseline;
- measurable desired effect;
- success and reversal thresholds;
- observation period;
- independent outcome adjudicator;
- procurement/commercial status when applicable.

## Gate rule
Until the mandatory evidence is present, Architectonic may support discovery, scoping, synthetic demonstration, and proposal development only. No field outcome, calibration record, client result, effectiveness claim, or commercial conversion may be invented.
