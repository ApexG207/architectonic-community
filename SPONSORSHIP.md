Architectonic's public Community Preview is provided to support evaluation, education, research, technical discussion, and design-partner discovery.

## Cash App

Support helps fund continued product development, testing, documentation, security review, synthetic evaluation assets, and community tooling.

## Important licensing boundary

Payments or sponsorships are voluntary support and do **not** grant:

- ownership or equity;
- commercial-use rights;
- production-deployment rights;
- sublicensing or redistribution rights;
- rights to proprietary Architectonic Enterprise components;
- certification, endorsement, governance authority, or professional services;
- access to customer-specific, government, or restricted environments.

Commercial, enterprise, OEM, government, regulated-environment, or production use requires a separate written license or services agreement with Apex Governance Group.

## Tax notice

Support should not be represented as tax-deductible charitable giving unless Apex Governance Group has separately established and documented the legal basis for that treatment.
